How the trial and subscription work

SDP is free to download. Your first 14 days are free — no card, nothing to cancel. After that, SDP is $7.99 a month through your Apple ID. If you stop, everything you put in stays yours: you can still open it, read it, and export it.

Your data

Your numbers live on your Mac and in your own iCloud. SDP has no accounts, no servers of its own, and no analytics. Read the privacy policy.
