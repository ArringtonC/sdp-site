How it works

Three things, and then once a month.

Put what you want on a wall

A trip, a kitchen, a car, a year with no debt. Give each one a photograph, so it stays a real thing rather than a number.

Give it your real numbers

What you have, what you owe, what you pay each month. You type them; D×M never reaches into a bank on your behalf.

Check in, once a month

Five minutes. D×M tells you what fits now, what's next, and what moved since last month — in sentences, not a dashboard.

What D×M never does

Some of this is the whole point.

Never colours money

No green, no red. A debt is not an emergency and a balance is not a verdict — it's a fact you're allowed to read calmly.

Never shows a false zero

If D×M doesn't know a figure, it says so. Unknown and nothing are different facts, and treating them the same is how money apps lie politely.

Never scores you

No streaks, no levels, no badges, no confetti. Your own money is not a game you can be losing.

Never takes your numbers

They live in your own iCloud and nowhere else. There is no D×M server holding them, no account to make, and nobody here who can read them.

Never connects to your bank

You type what you have. It takes a few minutes a month, and it means no credentials, no read-only token, and nothing to breach.

Questions

The ones worth asking first.

Does it connect to my bank?

No, and that's deliberate. You type your balances once a month. It takes a few minutes, it means there are no credentials to steal, and typing a number is a moment where you actually look at it — which is most of the value.

Where does my data live?

In your own iCloud, on the devices you sign in on. There is no D×M server holding your money and no account to create. Nobody here can read any of it — not because we promise not to, but because it never arrives.

Do I need a card for the free two weeks?

Yes. The free period is Apple's introductory offer, so it runs through your Apple ID with a payment method attached, and nothing is charged for two weeks. You cancel in your Apple settings like any other subscription.

What happens if I stop paying?

D×M goes read-only. Every figure, every month and every picture stays exactly where it is — you can open it, read it and export the whole record. It simply stops accepting new work. Locking someone out of their own financial history is taking it hostage, and we don't.

Is one membership enough for both my devices?

Yes. It belongs to your Apple ID, so buying on the Mac opens the iPhone and the other way round. Family Sharing is on, so your household is covered too.

Is this a budgeting app?

No. There are no envelopes, no spending categories and no monthly allowance to blow. D×M answers a different question — not "what did I spend", but "can this month carry the thing I actually want".
