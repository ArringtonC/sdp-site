D×M · privacy policy · 1 September 2026

Your numbers stay yours.

What D×M stores, and where

Everything you enter — accounts, balances, bills, debts, goals, photos, notes — is stored on your Mac and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud database. D×M has no user accounts and no servers of its own. We cannot see your data.

What D×M sends anywhere

Nothing, by default. D×M has no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising.

One optional feature, Leo's cloud voice, is off unless you turn it on in Settings. When it is on, the figures in a single answer — your totals, and the name, amount and timing of a goal Leo mentions — are sent to DeepSeek (api.deepseek.com) so Leo can phrase the answer. Nothing is sent when it is off, and you can turn it off at any time. Your API key is stored in the macOS Keychain, never in the app's files.

Find a photo. When you press "Find a photo" on a goal, the words in the search field are sent to Pexels (api.pexels.com) so it can show you pictures. Only your search words go — nothing about your money, and nothing that identifies you. The picture you choose is stored on your Mac like any other, with a small credit line naming the photographer, as Pexels asks.

Payments

The subscription is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple ID. D×M never sees your payment details.

Your rights

Delete the app and its data at any time. Export everything D×M knows from Settings at any time. Questions: arrington.copeland@gmail.com.

This policy changes only if the app's behaviour changes, and the date above changes with it.
